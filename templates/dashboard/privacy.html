Privacy & Data Governance

Last updated: July 06, 2026

1. Data Collection Scope

SpecPilot AI is a locally hosted/sandboxed platform. We do not transmit workspace schemas, databases design structures, or project scopes to third-party tracking portals. All databases records and logs are stored in your configured SQLite database instance.

2. LLM Services Integration

To generate specifications, prompt payloads are sent to Google's Gemini API endpoints. When using your custom API Key override, requests are governed by Google AI Studio's terms of service. No proprietary parameters are cached on public servers.

3. Session Cookies & Authentication

Local storage and session cookies are utilized to manage authentication states, custom API overrides, and theme configurations.
